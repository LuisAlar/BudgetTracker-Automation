# Summer 2026: The "Simulated Independence" Budget

This budget is tailored entirely around your goal to pay for your own specific daily essentials (Food, Subscriptions, Gas) and your desire to **simulate paying for rent** regardless of whether your parents help out or not. 

By keeping your "Simulated Rent" fixed in your budget, any rent that your parents *do* pay immediately becomes your future savings without you having to re-do your math!

---

## 1. Your Base Essentials (What YOU Pay For)
No matter what happens with rent, this is the core chunk of money that you will be spending to run your own life comfortably:
**Total Base Essentials: $180/week (21% of Net Pay)**
- **Groceries / Food:** $125/week ($500/month for high-quality groceries independently)
- **Gas / Transportation:** $40/week 
- **Subscriptions:** $15/week (Spotify, Netflix, software, etc.)

## 2. Your "Experiences" Fund (The Wants)
Because you have specific events like the World Cup and raves that don't happen identically every week, you should route **$130/week** into an overall "Experience Fund" ($520/month).

Here is how that monthly $520 covers your specific goals seamlessly:
**Total Experience Fund: $130/week (15% of Net Pay)**
- **Dining Out & New Spots ($200/mo):** Gives you room for 1-2 really nice meals out per week ($25-$40/spot).
- **The World Cup Bar Fund ($100/mo):** Mexico is guaranteed at least 3 games in the group stage. This allows ~$35-$40 per game for fun, drinks, and food at the bar.
- **Raves & Music Events ($120/mo):** Easily covers 2-3 underground/local artists per month ($15-$25), OR allows you to save up and blow it on 1 headliner artist/festival a month ($60-$80).
- **The "Za" & Hemp Wraps ($50/mo):** Covers your $30 pick-up (7gs) plus $20 leftover. Premium hemp blunt wraps (like High Hemp or King Palms) usually only cost about $1.50 to $3.00 for a pack of two, meaning that $20 easily keeps you set on papers/wraps for the month!
- **The "Spontaneous Fun" Fund ($50/mo):** Since you spent less on the Za, this $50 acts as a buffer for anything you haven't thought of yet! (e.g., an unexpected late-night movie run, buying a round for friends, paying a sudden club cover charge, or parking for an event).

***
**Money Remaining for Rent & Savings: $540/week (64% of Net Pay)**

## 3. The Scenarios: Simulating Rent

You mentioned you want to budget as if you are paying rent, so let's set a **"Housing Fund" at $190/week** (which equals the full $820/month rent). Here is how your remaining $540/week breaks down depending on how much your parents help.

### Scenario A: You Pay Rent in Full
*If your parents do not help with rent.*
- **To Landlord:** $190/week
- **To Your Savings:** $350/week
- **Outcome:** You afford full independence, have a stacked Experience Fund, and *still* save **$3,500** over a 10-week summer.

### Scenario B: Parents Pay Half Rent (Most Realistic)
*Your parents cover $410/mo, you cover $410/mo.*
- **To Landlord:** $95/week
- **To Your Savings:** $445/week (Your normal savings + the simulated rent money you didn't have to spend!)
- **Outcome:** You save **$4,450** over the summer while enjoying great food, raves, the Za, and the World Cup.

### Scenario C: Parents Cover Rent entirely
*If rent is fully covered and you only worry about your Base Essentials.*
- **To Landlord:** $0/week
- **To Your Savings:** $540/week
- **Outcome:** You save a massive **$5,400** over the summer!

---

## The Master Strategy
The beauty of this setup is that you don't have to change your behavior between these scenarios. You will **always act as if you are paying $190/week for rent**. 
Come payday, you simply slice off that $190: part of it goes to the Landlord, and whatever is left over goes straight to your Savings account!
